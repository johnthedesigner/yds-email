Are you ready for tonight's meeting?

Meet Australian Flower Farmer Jen Foster and Floral Designer Erica Downes

Sunday, April 7, 2024 from 6:45pm to 8:30 pm EDT

*arrive 15 minutes before to get settled and say hello.

Photos by Erica Downes & Jennifer Foster

What happens when a flower farmer with the goal of producing beautiful blooms sustainably meets a flower farmer with a talent for creating stunning floral designs? They join forces, teaming up to create a new business opportunity together through on farm experiences combined with floral design workshops.

YDS is very excited to have as our guest presenters and new dahlia friends, Jennifer Foster of Southern Highlands Flower Farm and Erica Downes of Erica's Garden Flowers from New South Wales, Australia. Jen is a sustainable flower farmer with a focus on peonies and dahlias, while Erica grows flowers for wedding design and floral arranging in her small private garden. These two amazing women have come together to build their separate businesses through a joint venture of Erica's floral design workshops plus Jen's Farm Experiences that combine and enhance their unique talents and passions.

We will meet Erica and Jen and hear their stories, plus enjoy a floral design demonstration by Erica along with a tour of Jen’s flower farm. Jen’s farm is truly a beautiful place, and Erica's floral designs are simply stunning. We have met Jen and Erica virtually and we can definitely say you will not want to miss this coming meeting! Save the date and together we’ll get a firsthand view of the special places Jen and Erica call home.
