The YDS Big Spring Meeting!

Sunday, May 5, 2024 from 11:00am to 2:00 pm ET

Photo by John Livornese

Spring is finally here, the weather is warming up and it’s the time of year we welcome the growing season with our fun annual club Spring meeting. Don’t miss out as we get time to chat with fellow members about what dahlias we are all excited to grow and maybe find yourself bringing home an extra tuber or two.

This time for our Big Spring meeting we will have time to ask dahlia growing questions among fellow member friends and share our favorite garden tips and preparations. There will be lots of Q&A and sharing time. During the meeting we’ll be sure to prepare new growers with confidence as we all are preparing to plant. As your planting dahlias are you also thinking about ways to stake your plants? We will share various ways on how to support your dahlias plants. So bring your growing questions, maybe those undivided dahlia clumps, and tips you can share so that we can have a fun afternoon learning from each other.

What would a Big Spring meeting be without fun tubers to auction off? Be there so you can participate in our 3rd annual tuber auction, door prizes and raffles. In addition, we know you’ve been waiting all winter and we’re excited to announce and share the YDS club tuber of the year. You must be there to get your free club tuber*! *We have limited supply so be sure to be there early.

Location: Spence Farm @ Hurld Wyman Elementary School, 41 Wyman Street, Woburn, MA 01801

Date & Time: Sunday, May 5, 2024 from 11:00am to 2:00pm

RSVP through SignUp Genius: An RSVP isn’t required but extremely helpful for us as we make plans. We want to have enough bagels and coffee so please take a moment and let us know you’re coming.
RSVP on SignUp Genius

Tuber Order PickUp: Last call for pick up!!! Members who have tuber orders that need to be picked up, be sure to attend as we will have your tuber order ready for pick up at the meeting.
